About

Hi, I'm Arjun. I created SciflyOnWheels (SOW) because I believe adventure isn't something you outgrow. It's something you pursue throughout your life.

Whether it's learning a new skill, playing your favorite sports or simply stepping outside your comfort zone, every adventure helps us grow stronger, healthier, and more confident.

SOW is a reminder to keep moving, keep exploring, and never stop challenging yourself.
